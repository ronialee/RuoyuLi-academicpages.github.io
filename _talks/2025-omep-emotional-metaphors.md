This study examines the relationship between gender, age, and preschoolers’ recognition of emotional metaphors in picture books. It analyzes the recognition performance of 30 children aged 3–6 across four types of emotional metaphors: character metaphors, expression–action metaphors, color metaphors, and natural-environment metaphors. The results show significant gender differences in the recognition of character and expression–action metaphors, with a significant interaction between gender and age for character metaphors. In contrast, no significant effects of age, gender, or their interaction were found for color or natural-environment metaphors.
