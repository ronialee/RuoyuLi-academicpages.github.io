Completed approximately 12 months of thesis-related fieldwork and teaching practicums across rural, county-level, and metropolitan kindergarten settings between 2021 and 2025.

The work included field observations and interviews for undergraduate and master's research projects, with experience in qualitative data collection and research with young children. Working in kindergartens serving communities with different institutional resources and family backgrounds also provided first-hand insight into variation in children's educational environments and experiences.
